How Alfredo works

It hands you the recommendation, then grades it.

Dashboards show you charts and leave the thinking to you. Alfredo reads your existing POS system and hands you a short list of recommendations worth trying. When you try one, it measures what actually happened and tells you straight whether it worked.

No new hardware, nothing to install, no staff retraining. Alfredo connects to the POS system you already run.

Small, careful, and the same menu for everyone.

A trial in Alfredo is one item, one change, for a set number of weeks. Every guest sees the same menu; results are compared against your own history and a control item, never against your customers. If a change backfires, it rolls back in minutes, and the board says so. This is how you were already improving your menu, just measured honestly.

Does your website still match your till?

Prices change at the till and the website never hears about it, or the other way around. Customers see one price and pay another, and nobody notices because cross checking them is nobody's job. Alfredo compares your published menu against what customers actually pay and flags every gap with the evidence: in real client data it found website prices that had been stale for months, one off by 40 to 50 percent, and one website price the till had never started charging. It never changes a price anywhere. It flags; you decide.

With cost data connected, every recommendation on the board argues from real margins: a price change shows cost, price, and margin so you can see it is safe; a promotion states what the giveaway costs and the exact volume needed to break even; a cross sell states the margin each attachment adds. The same add on data doubles as menu development evidence: which extras your guests already vote for with their orders, and what each one earns, for when you are deciding what to build next. Alfredo recommends and measures. You decide.

Customer intelligence

Your regulars, found in your own data.

Alfredo works out who your confirmed guests and regulars are from the data you already have: your loyalty program if you run one, and the transaction data itself if you do not. Either way, no new app to launch and no sign up sheet at the host stand. See who keeps the lights on, how regulars differ from occasional visitors, and which patterns deserve a nudge.

And in keeping with the numbers rule: only confirmed guests are counted. Alfredo never inflates the panel with estimated crowd sizes.
