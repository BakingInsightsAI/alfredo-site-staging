Questions

The things operators ask first.

Which POS systems does Alfredo support?

Toast and Clover today. If you run a different system, get in touch and we will tell you honestly whether yours is ready yet.

Is there a contract?

Month to month. Leave whenever you want. We would rather earn the next month than lock you into it.

What counts as a location?

A physical place of business: one restaurant, one taproom, one truck. The number of registers or terminals inside it does not matter. A restaurant with three registers is still one location.

Does Alfredo know what my dishes cost to make?

If you share your recipe and ingredient costs, yes: Alfredo connects them to your sales and adds margin intelligence on top, at no extra charge. If you do not have cost data, everything else works without it.
