The numbers rule

We do not make up numbers.

Most analytics tools try to impress you with big projected numbers. Alfredo does the opposite.

If the data is not there, the number is not there either. Here is the whole promise, in public, so you can hold us to it.

Our four promises

  • 01

    No invented figures

    Every dollar comes from your own sales. If we cannot trace it, you do not see it.

  • 02

    Pending means pending

    When there is not enough data yet, Alfredo says so. It never dresses up a guess with decimals.

  • 03

    Verdicts, not victory laps

    Every test ends one of three ways: it worked, it did nothing, or it backfired. When it backfires, Alfredo says so.

  • 04

    No peeking

    Results stay hidden until a test is finished, so nobody calls it early. Not even us.

Why we built it

There are tools that report, tools that do your books, and tools that forecast. None of them suggested what to try and then admitted when they were wrong. So we built one.
