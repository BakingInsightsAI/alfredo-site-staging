Questions and answers

What owners ask about Alfredo.

Short answers, in the words owners use. If something here is not clear, ask us directly and we will tell you honestly.

  • What is Alfredo?

    Alfredo by BakingInsightsAI is software that reads the sales you already ring through your POS and turns them into a short weekly list of changes worth trying. Each suggestion comes with why it came up, how much work it is, what it risks, how to do it, and when to check back. You decide what to try, and Alfredo comes back with an honest answer on whether it worked, did nothing, or backfired.

  • Who is Alfredo for?

    Alfredo is for restaurants, bars, breweries, cafes and bakeries that ring their sales through a POS. It is built for the owner or manager who makes the decisions, not for a team of analysts. It has been tested with a brewpub and is live across a 14 location restaurant chain.

  • What does Alfredo do with my sales data?

    Alfredo reads your POS sales and turns what it finds into a few changes worth trying each week. It also looks at your menu, your guests as far as your POS records who is who, and what the places down the street list publicly. Every figure it reports comes from your own sales, and if it cannot trace a number you do not see it.

  • Does Alfredo work with my POS?

    Alfredo connects to the POS system you already use, with no new hardware and no staff training. It can also bring in information from other tools you rely on, such as your recipe and ingredient costs. Tell us what you run, and we will confirm your setup on the demo call.

  • How much does Alfredo cost?

    Counter is $99 a month for one location, House is $199 a month for one to three locations, and Group is $299 a month for four to ten. That is one price for your whole business, not per location and not per user, with no setup fee and month to month. If you run more than ten locations, talk to us and we will put together an arrangement that fits your group.

  • Is there a free trial or founding offer?

    The first 10 businesses get every feature, with no limits, free for six months, and 9 of 10 spots are still open. However many locations you run. After that you pay the price for your size, month to month, and that price stays locked for a full year.

  • How is Alfredo different from a restaurant analytics dashboard?

    A dashboard shows you what happened and leaves the next move to you. Alfredo suggests what to try, as one short list each week, and then comes back with a verdict: it worked, it did nothing, or it backfired. Results stay hidden until a test is finished, so nobody calls it early.

  • Can Alfredo help with menu engineering and food costs?

    Yes, once you share your recipes and ingredient costs. With those, Alfredo shows what each plate makes after the extras are counted, which items are selling below what they cost to make, and which ingredient is squeezing a plate. Without them, everything else still works.

  • How do I know if a change worked?

    Every test ends one of three ways: it worked, it did nothing, or it backfired. You choose when to check back, and Alfredo suggests a date you can make shorter or longer. If the time you chose is not enough to call it, Alfredo says so rather than guess.

  • Does Alfredo change my menu or prices on its own?

    No. Alfredo recommends and you decide. Nothing on your menu moves until you say so.

  • How long does setup take?

    About two weeks. You request a demo and we reply within one business day, then a 30 minute call shows you Alfredo and what you would get from it. If you want to go ahead, we plan the setup together and connect your POS, with no new hardware and no staff training.

  • Is my data private?

    Yes. Your data is private to your business. Only you and the Alfredo team can see it, and it is never used for any other customer or to train AI models.
