The numbers rule

We do not make up numbers.

Plenty of analytics tools try to impress you with big projected numbers. Alfredo does the opposite.

If the data is not there, the number is not there either. Here is the whole promise, in public, so you can hold us to it.

There are tools that report, tools that do your books, and tools that forecast. We could not find one that suggested what to try and then admitted when it was wrong. So we built one.

The three answers

A good idea is useful. An honest answer is better.

Every test Alfredo reports on ends as one of three words, and each of them means the same thing every time.

Worked.
The change helped, on the information available.
No effect.
Nothing moved enough to call it a difference.
Backfired.
The change made things worse, and Alfredo says so.

Alfredo recommends. You decide.

Illustrative examples

Worked

Fifty cents off the House Lager

Three weeks later the bar took $247 more a week. After what you would have made anyway.

No effect

Calamari moved to the top of the starters

It sold the same. Alfredo said so, and the top spot went to something that does move.

Backfired

Happy hour pushed an hour later

The early crowd did not come back and the later hour did not fill. It went back the same night.

If the time you chose is not enough to call it, Alfredo says so rather than guess.

Our four promises

  • 01

    No invented figures

    Every dollar comes from your own sales. If we cannot trace it, you do not see it.

  • 02

    Pending means pending

    When there is not enough data yet, Alfredo says so. It never dresses up a guess with decimals.

  • 03

    Verdicts, not victory laps

    Every test ends one of three ways: it worked, it did nothing, or it backfired. When it backfires, Alfredo says so.

  • 04

    No peeking

    Results stay hidden until a test is finished, so nobody calls it early. Not even us.

The examples on this website are labeled and invented, to show you how Alfredo behaves. The numbers inside Alfredo come from your own sales and follow the four promises above.
