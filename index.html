Meet Alfredo: the in house consultant your business never had.

Every week Alfredo reads your own transaction data and hands you a short list of small changes worth trying, the way a good consultant would, except it is there every week and it measures what actually happened. We do not inflate numbers. We do not estimate around gaps. If the data is not there, the number is not there either.

Our philosophy is simple: no drastic changes. You know your business better than any outsider ever will. Alfredo's job is to hand you the information for small, low cost changes, help you try them, and stack incremental gains that hold up over time.

What Alfredo does

Every analytics tool shows you what happened. Alfredo tells you what to do about it.

Not a dashboard. A weekly to do list that grades itself. Alfredo runs a loop: it reads your POS, ranks its recommendations, you pick one, it measures the outcome, and the result trains next week's board. Four kinds of recommendations, one ranked list.
