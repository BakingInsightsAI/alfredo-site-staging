    FAQ

    Questions, answered straight.

    Which POS systems does Alfredo work with?

    Alfredo works with the POS you already run. Tell us which one, and we will tell you honestly whether yours is ready yet.

    Do I need to install anything?

    No. No new hardware, nothing to install at the register, and no retraining for your staff. Alfredo connects to the POS you already run.

    Will Alfredo tell me to overhaul my menu?

    No drastic changes. You know your business better than any outsider ever will. Alfredo hands you small, low cost changes, helps you try them, and stacks incremental gains that hold up over time.

    What happens when the data is not there?

    We do not inflate numbers. We do not estimate around gaps. If the data is not there, the number is not there either.

    Who is Alfredo for?

    Independent food and beverage businesses: restaurants, bars, cafes, bakeries, and food trucks, from single locations to multi location groups.

    Is it actually live?

    Yes. Tested with a brewpub and live across a 14 location restaurant chain, with real transactions, live weekly boards, and real trials with real verdicts.
