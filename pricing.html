Questions owners ask

The practical details.

Which POS systems are supported?

Toast and Clover are supported today. If you use another POS, ask us about compatibility and export options.

Is there a contract?

No long term contract. Plans are month to month.

What counts as a location?

One physical business location. Multiple registers at one venue still count as one location.

Can Alfredo calculate dish costs?

Yes, when you provide recipe and ingredient costs. Other features do not require those inputs.
