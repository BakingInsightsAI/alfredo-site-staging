Questions

What owners ask first.

Which POS systems does Alfredo support?

Toast and Clover today. If you run a different system, get in touch and we will tell you honestly whether yours is ready yet.

Is there a contract?

No long term contract. Month to month. We would rather earn the next month than lock you in.

What counts as a location?

One physical place: one restaurant, one taproom, one truck. Three registers in one restaurant is still one location.

Does Alfredo know what my dishes cost to make?

If you share your recipe and ingredient costs, yes, at no extra charge. If you do not, everything else still works.
