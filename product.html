The list itself

What a suggestion tells you before you decide.

Every one of them comes with the same six things, so you can judge it in a few seconds and get back to the floor.

  • What it is. The change itself, in one line you could read out to the floor.
  • Why it came up. What in your own sales put it on the list this week.
  • How much work. Low, medium or high, so a busy week gets the easy ones.
  • What it risks. What could go the other way, said plainly before you try it.
  • How to do it. The steps, and who needs to know.
  • When to check back. The date Alfredo comes back with the answer.

Ask it anythingThe way you would ask a manager

Illustrative example
  • How am I doing this week?

    Revenue is up 4.2% week over week, most of it from Friday night. Orders are flat and average check is up $1.10. One thing to watch: Monday is still your softest day by a wide margin.

  • What are my best sellers?

    Wings lead on volume, but your best seller is not your best earner: your highest margin mover is a mid shelf pour. Want the list sorted by profit instead of popularity?

  • What do you recommend?

    Three things on this week's list: a small price trial on your mozzarella sticks, a pairing nudge for loaded nachos with your house IPA, and one competitor pattern worth reading. Want to start with the one worth the most?
