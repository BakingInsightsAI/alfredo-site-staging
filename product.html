What it does

One short list, every Monday.

Alfredo reads the sales you already ring through your existing POS system, then looks at your menu, your guests and the places down the street. Every Monday it turns that into three to five changes worth trying. You choose which ones to run as a test. It reports back.

Inside a suggestion

What a suggestion tells you before you decide.

Every one of them comes with the same six things, so you can judge it in a few seconds and get back to the floor.

  1. 01

    What it is

    The change itself, in one line you could read out to the floor.

  2. 02

    Why it came up

    What in your own sales put it on the list this week.

  3. 03

    How much work

    Low, medium or high, so a busy week gets the easy ones.

  4. 04

    What it risks

    What could go the other way, said plainly before you try it.

Beyond the list

Your menu, your guests, and what is happening nearby.

What every plate really earns.

Once the extras are counted, the plate that sells most is often not the plate that earns most. Alfredo separates the two, and flags a cost that has crept up before it eats the week. This part needs your recipes and ingredient costs.

  • What each plate makes once the extras are counted, from your own recipes and costs.
  • Items selling below what they cost to make.
  • The one ingredient squeezing a plate.
  • A real cost problem told apart from a false alarm.
  • Which items to show off, keep, or rethink.

Who comes in, and what is happening nearby.

New faces, the regulars, and the ones who have gone quiet, as far as your POS tells you who is who. Then the places down the street: the prices and hours they list publicly, and what their reviews say.

  • New, returning, and quiet regulars, where your POS records who is who.

Questions in plain words, answered from your own numbers.

No report to build and no chart to read. Ask the way you would ask a manager. The answers come from the numbers you have connected, and if Alfredo does not know, it says so.

Ask it anythingThe way you would ask a manager

Illustrative example
  • How am I doing this week?

    Revenue is up 4.2% week over week, most of it from Friday night. Orders are flat and average check is up $1.10. One thing to watch: Monday is still your softest day by a wide margin.

  • What are my best sellers?

    Wings lead on volume, but your best seller is not your best earner: your highest margin mover is a mid shelf pour. Want the list sorted by profit instead of popularity?

  • What do you recommend?

    Three things on this week's list: a small price cut on your mozzarella sticks, a pairing nudge for loaded nachos with your house IPA, and one competitor pattern worth reading. Want to start with the one worth the most?
