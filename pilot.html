How we work

A platform shaped to your business, not the average one.

Every venue runs differently, so Alfredo starts with yours. This is not a consultant who visits once, drops a binder, and disappears. We work alongside you, and the platform keeps measuring after the conversation ends. Restaurants, bars, cafes, bakeries, food trucks: if you ring sales through a POS, get in touch and we will tell you honestly where the fit stands today.

How we work with pilot partners

We start by listening

Onboarding begins with an intake questionnaire: your history, your goals, what you have already tried, what makes your floor different. Recommendations are tuned to your business, not a category average. Onboarding does not require new hardware, new software, or staff retraining. You connect your POS and Alfredo does the rest.

Your reputation is data

What the public says about you and the venues around you feeds the engine. If your regulars love the service and your neighbor's fries are suddenly famous, your weekly board knows.

Shaped to fit

The dashboard configures per venue: a brewery gets a tap room, a bar gets a bar program, and we are open to customization where your needs do not match the default. Early partners shape the roadmap.

Who built it

Alfredo was built by a founder with a decade of experience turning data and consumer behavior into growth strategy for companies, and by a multi location restaurant owner who partnered on the design from the first week. Every feature started as a real question that owner needed answered about his own rooms: which price to move, which pairing to push, which competitor was pulling regulars. The product was shaped around the way a restaurant is actually run, not around the way analytics software is usually sold. When you join as a pilot partner, you work directly with the founder, and your questions shape what gets built next.
